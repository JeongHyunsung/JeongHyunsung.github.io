auto-studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

auto-studio is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by Hyunsung Jeong. Its only purpose is to upload videos that its operator produced to YouTube channels that the operator owns. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the operator signs in to it.

Use of YouTube API Services

auto-studio uses YouTube API Services. By using auto-studio you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information handled through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

With the account owner's authorization, auto-studio uses Google OAuth to obtain access to the owner's YouTube channel in order to:

  • upload videos and set their title, description, tags, privacy status and thumbnail; and
  • read the channel's own basic information (such as its name) to confirm which channel was authorized.

auto-studio does not access any other Google account data, and does not access data about other YouTube users or viewers.

How data is used, stored and shared

  • The OAuth refresh token is stored only as an encrypted secret in the operator's private GitHub repository and is used only by the operator's own automated upload job.
  • auto-studio does not collect, sell, share or transfer personal information to any third party, and does not use data for advertising.
  • auto-studio does not use cookies or analytics, and does not store any YouTube data beyond the IDs and URLs of the videos it uploaded.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings (security settings). After revocation the stored token stops working and is deleted from the repository's secrets.

Changes and contact

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent to the operator via github.com/JeongHyunsung.
